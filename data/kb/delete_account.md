# Deleting your account
Category: account

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and all of your data.

1. Export anything you want to keep first (see "Exporting your data").
2. Cancel any active paid subscription under Settings > Billing > Plan.
3. Open Settings > Data & Privacy and click "Delete account".
4. Type your email address to confirm.

Deletion is scheduled for 14 days later; sign in during that time to cancel it. After 14 days the data is permanently erased and cannot be recovered. Workspace owners must transfer ownership or delete the workspace before deleting their own account. Formal GDPR erasure requests with legal documentation are handled by our privacy team.
