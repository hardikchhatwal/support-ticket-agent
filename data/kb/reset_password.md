# How to reset your password
Category: account

If you cannot sign in, you can reset your password yourself without contacting support.

1. Go to the sign-in page and click "Forgot password".
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link. The link is valid for 30 minutes.
4. Open the link and choose a new password of at least 12 characters.
5. Sign in with your new password.

If the reset email does not arrive within 5 minutes, check your spam folder and confirm you entered the correct email. Reset links expire after 30 minutes; request a new one if yours has expired.
