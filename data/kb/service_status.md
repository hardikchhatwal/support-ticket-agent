# Outages and the service status page
Category: technical

Our live system status, including incidents and scheduled maintenance, is published at status.example.com. You can subscribe there to email or SMS updates.

Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 72 hours ahead and runs on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.

If you suspect an outage, first check the status page. If an incident is already listed, updates are posted there every 30 minutes until it is resolved and you do not need to open a ticket. If the status page shows everything operational but you are seeing widespread errors (for example HTTP 500s on many requests, or the whole app being unavailable), that is a production incident and must be escalated to our on-call engineering team immediately. Business and Enterprise plans have a 99.9% uptime SLA; SLA credits are requested through billing after the incident.
