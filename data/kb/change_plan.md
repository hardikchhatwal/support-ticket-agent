# Upgrading or downgrading your plan
Category: billing

You can change your subscription plan at any time from billing settings.

1. Sign in and open Settings > Billing > Plan.
2. Click "Change plan" and pick the new tier.
3. Review the prorated amount, then confirm.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period so you keep paid features until then. Your plan change never deletes your data.
