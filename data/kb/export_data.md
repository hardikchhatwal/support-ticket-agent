# Exporting your data
Category: technical

You can export your data to CSV or JSON at any time.

1. Sign in and open Settings > Data & Privacy.
2. Click "Export data".
3. Choose CSV or JSON and the date range.
4. Submit the request. Large exports are prepared in the background.
5. You will receive an email with a download link when the export is ready. The link is valid for 24 hours.

Exports typically finish within a few minutes but can take up to an hour for large accounts. If your export has not arrived after an hour, request it again.
