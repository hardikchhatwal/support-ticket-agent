# Submitting feature requests and product feedback
Category: feature_request

We welcome ideas for new features.

1. Open Settings > Help > "Share feedback", or visit the public roadmap at roadmap.example.com.
2. Search for an existing request and upvote it, or create a new one.
3. Describe the problem you are trying to solve, not only the solution.

Our product team reviews every request. We cannot commit to delivery dates for individual requests, but items marked "Planned" or "In progress" on the roadmap are being worked on, and you will be emailed when a request you voted on ships. Support agents cannot add features on request.
