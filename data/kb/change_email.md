# Changing your account email address
Category: account

You can change the email address you use to sign in.

1. Sign in and open Settings > Profile.
2. Click "Change email" and enter the new address.
3. Enter your current password to confirm.
4. Click the verification link sent to the new address within 24 hours.

Until you verify, your old email keeps working. Invoices and notifications go to the new address once it is verified. If you no longer have access to the old email and cannot sign in, the change cannot be self-served and must be handled by our account team after identity verification. Accounts managed by SSO must change their email through their company identity provider.
