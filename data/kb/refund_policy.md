# Refund policy and how to request a refund
Category: billing

We offer a full refund within 14 days of a charge for monthly plans and within 30 days for annual plans.

1. Sign in and open Settings > Billing > Invoices.
2. Find the invoice you want refunded and click "Request refund".
3. Choose a reason and submit.

Eligible refunds are processed automatically and appear on your statement within 5-10 business days. Charges older than the refund window, or disputes involving chargebacks, cannot be self-served and require a billing specialist to review the account.
