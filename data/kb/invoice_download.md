# Downloading invoices and receipts
Category: billing

You can download any past invoice or receipt for your records.

1. Sign in and open Settings > Billing > Invoices.
2. Find the invoice by date.
3. Click "Download PDF".

Invoices include your billing address and any VAT/tax details on file. To change the company name, address, or tax ID that appears on invoices, update them under Settings > Billing > Business details before generating a new invoice.
