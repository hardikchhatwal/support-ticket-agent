# Changes not syncing between devices
Category: technical

If edits made on one device do not appear on another, work through these steps.

1. Confirm both devices are online and signed in to the same account and workspace.
2. Pull to refresh (mobile) or press Ctrl/Cmd + R (desktop and web).
3. Check the sync indicator in the bottom-left corner; a red icon means changes are waiting to upload.
4. Update both apps to the latest version.
5. Sign out and back in to force a full re-sync.

Offline edits sync automatically when the device reconnects. If two devices edited the same item offline, both versions are kept and the older one is saved under Version history. Changes that never appear after a full re-sync, or items that disappear, indicate a sync bug that engineering needs to investigate.
