# Student, nonprofit and annual discounts
Category: billing

We offer the following discounts:

- Annual billing: 2 months free (about 17% off) compared with paying monthly. Switch under Settings > Billing > Plan > "Switch to annual".
- Students and educators: 50% off the Pro plan with a valid school email address. Apply at Settings > Billing > Discounts > "Education".
- Registered nonprofits: 30% off any paid plan. Apply at Settings > Billing > Discounts > "Nonprofit" and upload proof of registration; applications are reviewed within 5 business days.

Discounts cannot be combined, except that education and nonprofit discounts can be applied to annual billing. Discounts apply from the next invoice and are not applied retroactively to past charges. Custom or volume pricing for more than 50 seats is handled by our sales team.
