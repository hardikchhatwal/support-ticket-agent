# Enabling two-factor authentication (2FA)
Category: account

Two-factor authentication adds a second step when signing in and is strongly recommended.

1. Sign in and open Settings > Security.
2. Click "Enable two-factor authentication".
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy.
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.
5. Save your backup codes somewhere safe.

If you lose access to your authenticator app, use a saved backup code to sign in, then re-enroll a new device. If you have lost both your device and your backup codes, account recovery must be handled by our account team.
