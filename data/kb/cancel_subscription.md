# Cancelling your subscription
Category: billing

You can cancel your subscription yourself; there is no need to contact support.

1. Sign in and open Settings > Billing > Plan.
2. Click "Cancel subscription".
3. Confirm the cancellation.

Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, after which it will not renew. You keep access to your data on the free tier. You will not be charged again once cancellation is confirmed. If you were charged after cancelling, see the refund policy article.
