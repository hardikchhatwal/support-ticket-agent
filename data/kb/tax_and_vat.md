# Sales tax, VAT and tax exemption
Category: billing

We charge sales tax or VAT based on the billing address on your account.

1. Sign in and open Settings > Billing > Business details.
2. Enter your company name, billing address and VAT/GST/tax ID.
3. Save. Future invoices will include the tax ID and apply reverse-charge VAT where eligible (EU and UK businesses with a valid VAT number).

Tax changes apply to future invoices only; past invoices cannot be reissued automatically. US tax-exempt organisations must upload an exemption certificate under Settings > Billing > Business details > Tax exemption; certificates are reviewed by our billing team within 3 business days. For W-9 or other vendor forms, a billing specialist must provide them.
