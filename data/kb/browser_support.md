# Supported browsers and web app loading problems
Category: technical

The web app supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported.

If the web app shows a blank page, loads slowly or buttons do not respond:
1. Hard refresh with Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R.
2. Try a private/incognito window to rule out extensions; ad blockers and privacy extensions are the most common cause.
3. Clear cookies and site data for our domain.
4. Update your browser to the latest version.

If the problem only happens on a company network, ask your IT team to allow our domains and WebSocket connections. If it happens in every browser and network, check our status page for an ongoing incident.
