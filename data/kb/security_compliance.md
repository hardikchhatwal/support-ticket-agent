# Security, privacy and compliance
Category: general

- Certifications: we are SOC 2 Type II certified and ISO 27001 certified. Reports are available under NDA from Settings > Data & Privacy > Compliance documents on Business and Enterprise plans.
- Encryption: data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2+ and at rest with AES-256.
- Data location: data is stored in the US by default. Enterprise customers can choose EU (Frankfurt) data residency at onboarding.
- GDPR: we act as a data processor. Our Data Processing Agreement (DPA) can be signed electronically from Settings > Data & Privacy > DPA.
- Backups: taken daily and kept for 30 days.

Security questionnaires, penetration test reports, custom DPAs and data residency migrations for existing accounts are handled by our security team.
