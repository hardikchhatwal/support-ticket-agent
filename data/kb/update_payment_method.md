# Updating your payment method
Category: billing

You can change the credit card or payment method on file at any time from your account.

1. Sign in and open Settings > Billing.
2. Click "Payment methods".
3. Add a new card, then set it as the default.
4. Remove the old card if you no longer want it on file.

Your next invoice will automatically charge the default payment method. Changing your card does not change your billing date or plan. If a payment previously failed, updating the card will retry the charge within a few hours.
