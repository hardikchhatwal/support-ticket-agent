# Available integrations
Category: feature_request

The following integrations are available today under Settings > Integrations:

- Slack: notifications for mentions, assignments and due dates.
- Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar: two-way sync of due dates.
- Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive: attach files from cloud storage.
- GitHub and GitLab: link commits and pull requests to items.
- Zapier: connect to 5,000+ other apps without code.

To connect one, open Settings > Integrations, click "Connect", and authorise access. Integrations not listed here are not available; requests for new integrations are feature requests for our product team. Microsoft Teams and Jira integrations are on the public roadmap but have no release date.
