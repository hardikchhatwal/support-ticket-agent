# Contacting support and response times
Category: general

You can reach support by email at support@example.com or from Settings > Help > "Contact support".

Support hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 UTC. Target first-response times are:
- Free plan: 2 business days.
- Pro plan: 1 business day.
- Business and Enterprise plans: 4 business hours, with 24/7 coverage for urgent incidents.

Live chat is available to Business and Enterprise plans during support hours. Phone support is available only to Enterprise customers through their account manager. Include your workspace name and any error messages or screenshots to help us respond faster.
