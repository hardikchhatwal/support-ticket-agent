# Adding and removing team seats
Category: billing

Paid plans are billed per seat (per active team member).

1. Sign in as a workspace admin and open Settings > Team.
2. Click "Invite member" to add a seat, or open a member and click "Remove" to free one.
3. Review the prorated charge or credit, then confirm.

Adding a seat is charged immediately, prorated for the rest of the billing period. Removing a seat gives a prorated credit on your next invoice, not a refund to your card. Only workspace admins can add or remove seats. Removing a member does not delete the content they created; ownership moves to the admin who removed them.
