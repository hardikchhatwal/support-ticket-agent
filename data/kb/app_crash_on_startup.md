# App crashes or freezes on startup
Category: technical

If the desktop or mobile app crashes immediately on launch, these steps resolve the most common causes.

1. Force-quit the app fully and reopen it.
2. Confirm you are on the latest version; update from the app store if not.
3. Clear the app cache: Settings > Advanced > Clear cache.
4. Restart your device.
5. If it still crashes, reinstall the app.

Most startup crashes are caused by an outdated version or a corrupted cache and are fixed by updating and clearing the cache. Crashes that persist after a clean reinstall, or that started right after a specific update for many users, indicate a bug that engineering needs to investigate.
