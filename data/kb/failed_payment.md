# Failed or declined payments
Category: billing

If a subscription payment fails, we email you and retry the charge automatically.

1. Check the email we sent for the decline reason (for example expired card or insufficient funds).
2. Sign in and open Settings > Billing > Payment methods.
3. Add a valid card and set it as the default, or update the expiry date on the existing card.
4. We retry the charge within a few hours of the update. You can also click "Retry payment" on the unpaid invoice.

We retry failed payments three times over 7 days. Your account keeps full access during this grace period. If all retries fail, the account is moved to the free tier; no data is deleted, and paid features return as soon as a payment succeeds. If your bank shows the charge as completed but we show it as failed, or you were charged more than once, a billing specialist needs to review the account.
